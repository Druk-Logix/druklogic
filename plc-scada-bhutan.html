PLC and SCADA project questions

Can Druk Logic upgrade an existing PLC or SCADA system?

Yes. We review your existing controller, field signals and operating sequences before planning the upgrade. We work with platforms including Siemens, Delta, Schneider and SICAM. For a legacy PLC replacement, we test the new logic against simulated inputs and faults before the planned shutdown.

What is the difference between FAT and SAT?

A factory acceptance test (FAT) checks control logic, interlocks and alarms before installation. A site acceptance test (SAT) checks the installed system and its field signals during commissioning. Our live pump control simulator demonstrates FAT checks for cases such as a motor overload and high tank level.

What documentation is available at handover?

Depending on the scope, handover can include the functional design specification, input and output list, documented PLC program and backup, SCADA or HMI screens, test records and as-built panel drawings.
