SCADA & HMI integration in Bhutan

Druk Logic integrates supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) systems and local human-machine interfaces (HMIs) for hydropower, water and manufacturing sites across Bhutan. We build operator screens, connect plant signals and test how the system reports faults.

Discuss your SCADA project

Operator screens, alarms and reports

We agree with your operators on what they need to see during a normal shift, at start-up and when a fault occurs. That list guides the screens, alarm priorities and reports we configure.

Operator displays and tag mapping

We map controller signals to screen tags, check names, units and ranges, and display equipment status and process values on plant overview and equipment screens.

Alarms and events

We document which conditions raise alarms, their priorities and what operators should see when an alarm is acknowledged or clears. An event history helps the next shift see when a fault began.

Trends and shift reports

We agree which variables and events to record. Operators can review trends such as tank level or pump runtime and use reports to hand over recurring faults and operating changes.

Upgrading an existing SCADA or HMI system

We review your active controllers, available project backups, display hardware and software licences. We identify which PLCs, workstations and existing screens can be reused, then agree what to change and which tests to run before and after the cutover.

SCADA for hydropower, water and manufacturing

Hydropower

Display auxiliary equipment status and process readings, with an event history that helps operators review trips and changes in operating state.

Water and pump stations

Show tank levels, flow, pressure and pump operation. Trends help operators see when falls in level or pressure began and how long pumps were running.

Manufacturing

Summarise machine status, active alarms and production stops across a line. Shift reports help operating teams flag recurring faults and hand over open issues.

Acceptance testing and handover

Factory acceptance testing

Before site work, we use simulated signals to review tag mapping, screen changes, alarm history and trends with your team. The test record captures agreed cases and any items still to resolve.

Site acceptance testing

During commissioning, we compare the display with field readings and check that agreed alarms and data history behave as expected. Handover can include the project backup, tag list, alarm matrix and test records.
